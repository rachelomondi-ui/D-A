The business scenario

Nova Retail wants to understand completed sales, customer value, and changes in monthly sales. All amounts are in USD. Cancelled orders are excluded from completed sales. Refunds, tax, and delivery fees are outside this example.

Table grain: each customer row represents one customer; each order row represents one order. customers.customer_id is unique. orders.customer_id links an order to its customer.
